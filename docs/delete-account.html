Delete Account

Warning: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently deleted from our servers.

How to Delete Your Account

Step 1: Open the MintyPOS app on your device and log in to your account.

Step 2: Navigate to the Profile section from the main menu or drawer.

Step 3: Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" option.

Step 4: Tap on "Delete Account" and confirm your decision when prompted.

Step 5: Enter your password to verify your identity and complete the deletion process.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • All your personal data will be permanently deleted
  • Your business data (products, inventory, sales history) will be removed
  • You will lose access to all MintyPOS features
  • Your account cannot be recovered after deletion

Data Retention

After account deletion, we retain certain data for legal and security purposes:

  • Transaction logs for 7 years (as required by law)
  • Security and fraud prevention records
  • Legal compliance records

Before You Delete

Consider these alternatives before deleting your account:

  • Export your data if you need it for records
  • Contact support if you're experiencing issues
  • Deactivate your account temporarily if you plan to return

Need Help?

If you have any questions or need assistance with account deletion, please contact our support team through the app or our official channels.
